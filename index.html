Publish your work

Your 5-step publishing tutorial

1
Scan or upload your work

For paper comics or books, scan or photograph each page clearly. If your work is digital, prepare the file on your device.

2
Upload it to your drive

Save the scanned or downloaded files to your cloud drive (such as Google Drive) or a folder on your device so you have a safe copy.

3
Upload your work to Crafix

Choose the file below and add its title and format. In this prototype, the file is selected locally and is not sent to a server.

4
Write a summary

Explain the premise, main character, their goal, and the central conflict. Help the reviewer understand your story.

5
Get your AI review

Submit your title and summary for feedback. The target score is 8/10 or higher; below 8/10 means revise and resubmit. Passing does not guarantee Crafix approval.
